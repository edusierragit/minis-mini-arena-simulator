# Privacy

Mini's Mini Arena Simulator stores keybinds and settings only in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to the developer or the analytics provider.

When analytics are enabled, the site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) to collect anonymous, aggregate usage information without cookies or persistent personal identifiers:

- page visits and referring site;
- approximate country, browser, and screen size;
- selected playable class;
- practice sessions started, restarted, and completed;
- selected difficulty and session length.

The game does **not** send configured keys or mouse buttons, player names, answers, scores, reaction times, or local storage contents.

The statistics are used only to understand adoption and improve the trainer. See [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for details about how the analytics service processes visits.
